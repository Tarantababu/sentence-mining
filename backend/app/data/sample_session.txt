SESSION 2026-11-20 — speaking class notes

You said: I do the school running every morning
Correct: I do the school run every morning
Why: "do the school run" is a fixed chunk, not "running"
RECURRING

You said: There is no point to wait
Correct: There's no point in waiting
Why: the pattern is "no point in + -ing"

You said: I made it by the hand
Correct: I made it by hand
Why: "by hand" takes no article

You said: I attended to the meeting
Correct: I attended the meeting
Why: "attend" takes no preposition for events
RECURRING

You said: The meeting was scheduled on Friday
Correct: The meeting is scheduled for Friday
Why: "schedule a meeting for" + day

You said: I got it for freely
Correct: I got it for free
Why: "for free" vs "freely" (adverb of manner)

You said: I wouldn't prefer to go
Correct: I'd rather not go
Why: "I'd rather not" vs "wouldn't prefer"

You said: I went to school by the bus
Correct: I went to school by bus
Why: zero article with "by + transport"; "the" is wrong here

VOCABULARY OF THE DAY
- school run: the daily trip taking kids to school (used well!)
- by hand: made without a machine

TRACKING LIST

- articles with institutions and transport

FOCUS FOR NEXT LESSON
